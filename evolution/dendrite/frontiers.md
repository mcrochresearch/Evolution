## Frontier Discovery Protocol

New frontiers are discovered through:
1. **Adjacent possible** — what's one step beyond current best strategies?
2. **Analogy** — what approaches work in similar domains?
3. **Inversion** — what's the opposite of everything we've tried?
4. **Combination** — can two partial solutions combine into a full one?
5. **Abstraction** — can we solve a more general version of the problem?
6. **External search** — what does documentation/web say about this type of problem?

---

_Frontiers keep evolution from stagnating. When fitness plateaus, explore a frontier._
