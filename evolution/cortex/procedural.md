# Procedural Memory

> Proven recipes — step-by-step procedures that reliably produce results.
> These are the agent's "muscle memory" — refined through repetition and success.
> Each recipe includes trigger conditions (WHEN to use) and success statistics.

## Recipe Promotion Protocol

A pattern becomes a recipe when:
1. It has been successfully applied 3+ times (from episodic memory)
2. It has a clear trigger condition (when to use it)
3. The steps can be articulated precisely
4. Success rate is above 60%

Recipes are the most valuable form of knowledge — they turn experience into repeatable skill.

### Recipe Format

```
## Recipe: [Name]
- **ID**: R[NNN]
- **When to Use**: [Trigger conditions — what situation calls for this recipe]
- **Prerequisites**: [What must be true before starting]
- **Steps**:
  1. [Step 1 — be specific and actionable]
  2. [Step 2]
  3. [Step N]
- **Verification**: [How to confirm the recipe worked]
- **Success Rate**: [X/Y attempts — e.g., 7/9]
- **Average Duration**: [How long this typically takes]
- **Failure Modes**: [Known ways this can go wrong and how to handle them]
- **Variations**: [Adaptations for different contexts]
- **Source Episodes**: [Which episodes this was distilled from]
- **Pheromone Strength**: [0.0-1.0 — stigmergic trail strength]
```
