# Hall of Fame

> The greatest strategies ever evolved — preserved for eternity.
> These are the elite — strategies that achieved exceptional fitness scores
> and represent peak performance for their respective challenges.

## Induction Criteria

A strategy enters the Hall of Fame when:
1. It achieves the highest fitness score ever recorded for its sub-goal
2. It successfully completes a sub-goal entirely
3. It demonstrates a novel approach that becomes a template for future strategies

Hall of Fame strategies are NEVER deleted. They serve as:
- **Templates** for future strategy genesis (seeding new populations)
- **Benchmarks** for measuring improvement
- **Transfer knowledge** applicable to different goals
