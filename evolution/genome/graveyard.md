# Strategy Graveyard

> Extinct strategies — approaches that were tried and failed.
> These are NOT deleted — they contain valuable negative knowledge.
> "What doesn't work" is as important as "what does."

## Resurrection Protocol

A graveyard strategy can be resurrected if:
1. The context has significantly changed since extinction
2. The strategy was killed by a now-fixed dependency
3. A new mutation idea could address the original failure mode
4. The metacognition engine identifies it as an unexplored frontier

When resurrected, the strategy re-enters the population with:
- Fitness reset to 0.0
- Generation incremented
- Lineage noting "Resurrected from graveyard"
- The original failure analysis preserved as a warning
