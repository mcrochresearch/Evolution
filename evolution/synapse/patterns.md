# Meta-Patterns

> Patterns about patterns — the metacognition layer.
> This is where the agent learns HOW it learns, discovers its own biases,
> and optimizes its learning process itself.
> Inspired by Schmidhuber's meta-learning (1987) and MAML (Finn et al., 2017).

## Pattern Mining Protocol

Every 5 cycles, analyze recent reflections for recurring themes:

### Questions to Ask
1. What types of changes tend to succeed? (positive pattern)
2. What types consistently fail? (anti-pattern)
3. Am I exploring enough, or stuck in a local optimum?
4. Am I spending too long on any one sub-goal?
5. Which mutation types produce the best offspring?
6. Am I getting faster at completing cycles?
7. Are my reflections getting more accurate? (predicted outcome vs actual)
