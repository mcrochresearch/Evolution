# Performance Analytics Skill

Frameworks for measuring, reporting, and optimizing marketing performance across channels and campaigns.

---

## Key Marketing Metrics by Channel

### Email Marketing

| Metric | Definition | Benchmark | What It Tells You |
|--------|-----------|-----------|-------------------|
| Delivery rate | Emails delivered / emails sent | 95-99% | List health and sender reputation |
| Open rate | Unique opens / emails delivered | 15-30% | Subject line and sender effectiveness |
| Click-through rate (CTR) | Unique clicks / emails delivered | 2-5% | Content relevance and CTA effectiveness |
| Click-to-open rate (CTOR) | Unique clicks / unique opens | 10-20% | Email content quality (for those who opened) |
| Unsubscribe rate | Unsubscribes / emails delivered | <0.5% | Content-audience fit and frequency tolerance |
| Bounce rate | Bounces / emails sent | <2% | List quality and data hygiene |
| Conversion rate | Conversions / emails delivered | 1-5% | End-to-end email effectiveness |
| Revenue per email | Total revenue / emails sent | Varies | Direct revenue attribution |
| List growth rate | (New subscribers - unsubscribes) / total list | 2-5%/mo | Audience building health |

### Social Media

| Metric | Definition | What It Tells You |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Impressions | Times content was displayed | Content distribution and reach |
| Reach | Unique users who saw content | Audience breadth |
| Engagement rate | (Likes + comments + shares) / reach | Content resonance |
| Click-through rate | Link clicks / impressions | Traffic driving effectiveness |
| Follower growth rate | Net new followers / total followers per period | Audience building |
| Share/Repost rate | Shares / reach | Content virality and advocacy |
| Video view rate | Views / impressions | Video hook effectiveness |
| Video completion rate | Completed views / total views | Video quality and length fit |
| Social share of voice | Your mentions / total category mentions | Brand visibility vs. competitors |

### Paid Advertising (Search and Social)

| Metric | Definition | What It Tells You |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Impressions | Times ad was shown | Budget utilization and targeting breadth |
| CTR | Clicks / impressions | Ad creative and targeting relevance |
| CPC | Total spend / clicks | Cost efficiency of traffic |
| CPM | Cost per 1,000 impressions | Awareness cost efficiency |
| Conversion rate | Conversions / clicks | Landing page and offer effectiveness |
| CPA | Total spend / conversions | Full-funnel cost efficiency |
| ROAS | Revenue / ad spend | Revenue generation efficiency |
| Quality Score | Google's relevance rating (1-10) | Ad-keyword-landing page alignment |
| Frequency | Avg times a user sees the ad | Ad fatigue risk |
| View-through conversions | Conversions from users who saw but did not click | Display/awareness influence |

### SEO / Organic Search

| Metric | Definition | What It Tells You |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Organic sessions | Visits from organic search | SEO effectiveness |
| Keyword rankings | Position for target keywords | Search visibility |
| Organic CTR | Clicks / impressions in search | Title and meta description effectiveness |
| Pages indexed | Pages in search index | Indexability and site health |
| Domain authority | Third-party authority score | Overall site strength |
| Backlinks | External sites linking to you | Content authority |
| Page load speed | Time to interactive | UX and ranking factor |
| Organic conversion rate | Organic conversions / organic sessions | Content quality and intent alignment |

### Content Marketing

| Metric | Definition | What It Tells You |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| Pageviews | Total views of content pages | Content reach |
| Unique visitors | Distinct users viewing content | Audience size |
| Avg time on page | Time spent on content | Engagement depth |
| Bounce rate | Single-page sessions / total sessions | Content-audience fit |
| Scroll depth | How far users scroll | Engagement through the piece |
| Social shares | Times content was shared | Resonance and virality |
| Backlinks earned | External links to content | Authority and SEO value |
| Lead generation | Leads attributed to content | Conversion effectiveness |
| Content ROI | Revenue attributed / production cost | Investment return |

### Overall Marketing / Pipeline

| Metric | Definition | What It Tells You |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| MQLs | Leads meeting marketing qualification criteria | Top-of-funnel effectiveness |
| SQLs | MQLs accepted by sales | Lead quality |
| MQL to SQL conversion rate | SQLs / MQLs | Marketing-sales alignment |
| Pipeline generated | Dollar value of opportunities created | Marketing impact on revenue |
| Pipeline velocity | How fast deals move through pipeline | Campaign urgency and quality |
| CAC | Total marketing + sales cost / new customers | Acquisition efficiency |
| CAC payback period | Months to recover CAC from revenue | Unit economics health |
| Marketing-sourced revenue | Revenue from marketing-originated deals | Direct contribution |
| Marketing-influenced revenue | Revenue from deals where marketing touched | Broader impact |

## Reporting Templates

### Weekly Marketing Report (Team Standups)
1. Top 3 metrics with week-over-week change
2. What worked this week (1-2 bullets with data)
3. What needs attention (1-2 bullets with data)
4. This week's priorities (3-5 action items)

### Monthly Marketing Report (Stakeholders)
1. Executive summary (3-5 sentences)
2. Key metrics dashboard (table with MoM and target comparison)
3. Channel-by-channel performance summary
4. Campaign highlights and results
5. What worked and what did not (with hypotheses)
6. Recommendations and next month priorities
7. Budget spend vs. plan

### Quarterly Business Review (Leadership)
1. Quarter performance vs. goals
2. Year-to-date trajectory
3. Channel ROI analysis
4. Campaign performance summary
5. Competitive and market observations
6. Strategic recommendations for next quarter
7. Budget request and allocation plan
8. Key experiments and learnings

### Dashboard Design Principles
- Lead with metrics that map to business objectives — not vanity metrics
- Show trends over time, not just point-in-time snapshots
- Include context: prior period, target, benchmark
- Color coding: green (on track), yellow (at risk), red (off track)
- Group by funnel stage or business question
- One page/screen — detail in appendix
- Update cadence matches decision cadence (real-time for paid, weekly for content)

## Trend Analysis and Forecasting

### Trend Identification
1. **Directional trends**: consistently up, down, or flat over 4+ periods?
2. **Inflection points**: where did direction change and what happened then?
3. **Seasonality**: predictable patterns by day/month/quarter?
4. **Anomalies**: one-time spikes or drops — repeatable or one-off?
5. **Leading indicators**: which metrics change first and predict future outcomes?

### Trend Analysis Process
1. Chart the metric over time (minimum 8-12 data points)
2. Identify overall direction (upward, downward, flat, cyclical)
3. Calculate rate of change (accelerating or decelerating?)
4. Overlay key events (campaigns, product changes, market events)
5. Compare to benchmarks or targets
6. Identify correlations with other metrics
7. Form causal hypotheses → plan tests to validate

### Simple Forecasting Approaches
- **Linear projection**: extend the current trend line (stable metrics)
- **Moving average**: average last 3-6 periods to smooth noise
- **Year-over-year**: last year's pattern adjusted for growth rate
- **Funnel math**: X leads × Y conversion rate = Z customers
- **Scenario modeling**: best / expected / worst case

### Forecasting Caveats
- Short-term (1-3 months) is more reliable than long-term
- Fewer than 12 data points = low confidence (flag it)
- External factors can invalidate trend-based forecasts
- Always present as ranges, not exact numbers

## Attribution Modeling

### Common Models

| Model | How It Works | Best For | Limitation |
|-------|-------------|----------|-----------|
| Last touch | 100% credit to last interaction | Understanding final conversion triggers | Ignores awareness and nurture |
| First touch | 100% credit to first interaction | Top-of-funnel effectiveness | Ignores nurture and conversion drivers |
| Linear | Equal credit to all touchpoints | Fair representation | Doesn't reflect relative impact |
| Time decay | More credit to closer touchpoints | Balanced, favoring recent | May undervalue awareness |
| Position-based (U-shaped) | 40% first, 40% last, 20% middle | Valuing discovery and conversion | Somewhat arbitrary weights |
| Data-driven | Algorithmic credit | Most accurate | Requires high conversion volume |

### Practical Guidance
- Start with **last-touch** if you have no model — simplest and most actionable
- Compare first-touch vs. last-touch to understand awareness vs. conversion channels
- Use **position-based** as a reasonable middle ground for most B2B
- Data-driven requires high conversion volume to be statistically meaningful
- Use attribution directionally, not as absolute truth
- Any model is better than no model

### Attribution Pitfalls
- Don't optimize one channel in isolation using single-touch attribution
- Awareness channels (display, social, PR) always look bad in last-touch models
- Conversion channels (search, retargeting) always look bad in first-touch models
- Self-reported attribution ("how did you hear about us?") is qualitative color only
- Cross-device and cross-channel gaps mean attribution is always incomplete

## Optimization Framework

### Optimization Process
1. **Identify**: which metrics are underperforming vs. target or benchmark?
2. **Diagnose**: where in the funnel? (impressions → clicks → conversions → retention)
3. **Hypothesize**: what's causing it? (audience, message, creative, offer, timing, technical)
4. **Prioritize**: biggest impact, least effort?
5. **Test**: design an experiment to validate the hypothesis
6. **Measure**: did the change improve the metric?
7. **Scale or iterate**: roll out wins; iterate on inconclusive or failed tests

### Optimization Levers by Funnel Stage

| Funnel Stage | Problem Signal | Optimization Levers |
|-------------|---------------|---------------------|
| Awareness | Low impressions, low reach | Budget, targeting, channel mix, creative format |
| Interest | Low CTR, low engagement | Ad creative, headlines, content hooks, audience |
| Consideration | High bounce, low time on page | Landing page content, speed, UX, relevance |
| Conversion | Low conversion rate | Offer, CTA, form length, trust signals |
| Retention | High churn, low repeat engagement | Onboarding, email nurture, product experience |

### Prioritization Matrix

**Impact × Effort → Priority:**

| | Low Effort | High Effort |
|---|-----------|------------|
| **High Impact** | Do immediately | Plan and resource |
| **Low Impact** | Do if capacity allows | Deprioritize |

### Testing Best Practices
- Test ONE variable at a time for clean results
- Define the success metric BEFORE launching
- Calculate required sample size before starting — don't end tests early
- Run minimum one full business cycle (typically 1 week for B2B)
- Document all tests and results — failed tests are valuable information
- Share learnings across the team
- Confirming the status quo is not a failure — it builds confidence

### Continuous Optimization Cadence
- **Daily**: paid campaigns — budget pacing, anomalies, disapproved ads
- **Weekly**: channel performance — pause underperformers, scale winners
- **Bi-weekly**: refresh ad creative, test new variants
- **Monthly**: full performance review, new optimization opportunities, updated forecasts
- **Quarterly**: strategic review of channel mix, budget allocation, targeting strategy
