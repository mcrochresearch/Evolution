# Micro-Research/Evolution Tracking

## Quick Start

1. Initialize with: `init_skill.py micro-research`
2. Add research protocols to `scripts/`
3. Document findings in `references/`

## Key Components

- **Evolutionary Metrics**: Track changes over time
- **Pattern Recognition**: Identify repetition/variation
- **Adaptation Analysis**: Measure response to environment
- **Selection Pressure**: Quantify survival/replication factors

## Usage Examples

- "Show me the evolutionary pattern in this biological system"
- "Analyze the adaptation of this technology over time"
- "Track the selection pressure in this ecosystem"
- "Compare mutation rates between species A and B"
- "Show adaptation trends in 5G network evolution"
